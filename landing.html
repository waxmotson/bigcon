REEL 01 / 11

THE TURN · EVERYONE STARTS AT QUIKSTOP

WALK IN. TAKE IT.

Every player opens with $15 and 2 Intel. Three steps along the red threads, then one action — and the obvious action is to seize whatever you are standing on.

INCOME
$3 salary plus everything you hold, at the start of every turn.
TOLLS
Rivals pay $1 to step onto your node. $2 if you hold Albanese; nothing if they hold James Bond.
THE MARKET
Each turn ends with one of 24 event cards. It pays someone, bills someone, and sets the mood for the whole table.

REEL 02 / 11

QANTAS · CORPORATE · $20

FLY THE JET

The most expensive exhibit on the board, because it deletes the board. Pay $3 mid-move and land on any node you like.

COST
$3, during movement. Landing ends your move; your action still follows.
SYSTEM
Distance stops mattering: the prized nodes are dealt far from Quikstop, and the jet owner reaches them first.
COUNTER
The Corporate Boeing can ground it for a round. A blockade closes the runway.

REEL 03 / 11

THE SISYPHUS BOULDER · WILD · $4

PUSH THE BOULDER

Stand on it and push. $10 the first time, then double for every turn you stay: $10, $20, $40, $80…

COST
Your action. No Cortisol.
THE CATCH
Walk off or lose the node and it rolls back to $10. Backroom Deals keeps the streak.
SYSTEM
A player camped on the hill isn’t seizing anything — and everyone knows exactly where to find them.

REEL 05 / 11

ASIC · ACCC · THE CORTISOL METER

CORTISOL 9: THE RAID

Every seizure is +1 Cortisol. A hostile takeover is +2. At nine the regulators come through the door, and the whole table watches.

THE DAMAGE
Your two highest-income nodes are seized. A fine of $5 or 30% of your cash. Intel burned down to 1. Next turn: one step, no income.
WHO PROFITS
The Hedge Fund collects $3 on every raid — even its owner’s.
STAY CALM
Spin, cook the books, lay low. Or raise everyone else’s Cortisol instead.

HOW IT PLAYS

A TURN, IN FOUR MOVES

Host a room, read out the buzzword, and up to eight players join from a browser or a Discord Activity. Short a friend? Seat a bot.

  1. INCOME

    $3 salary, plus your holdings, their synergies, and whatever the mood is doing to them.

  2. MOVE

    Three steps along the threads. More with a train, a shoe or a burned favour. Or just fly.

  3. ONE ACTION

    Seize, bug, leak, spin, audit, deal, push, launch. Intel ops are free on top.

  4. MARKET EVENT

    One card hits the whole table and moves the mood: calm, boom, panic or media frenzy.
